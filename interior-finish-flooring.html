Specialist Interior Finish & Flooring

We ensure every detail of your refurbishment is handled with precision, delivering a flawless finish that elevates the look and feel of your home.

Flawless Internal Finishing

Our finishing services bring your design vision to life, covering all internal aspects that create a beautiful, cohesive space. Our services include:

  • Professional plastering and skimming for smooth, paint-ready surfaces.
  • High-end decorative painting, staining, and wallpapering services.
  • Bespoke joinery for skirting, architraves, custom shelving, and built-in storage.
  • Detailed surface preparation and clean-up, ensuring minimal dust and disruption.

Expert Flooring Installation

The right flooring is essential for aesthetics and durability. We specialize in the correct preparation and installation of all major flooring types.

  • Installation of all wood types, including solid, engineered, and laminate flooring.
  • Precision tiling for floors in kitchens, bathrooms, hallways, and living areas.
  • Subfloor preparation, levelling, and repair to ensure a perfect foundation.
  • Integration of underfloor heating systems beneath tiled and stone floors.
